Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026 · Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alpha ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Meowgic mobile game (the "App"). If you have questions, contact us at supportvariable@gmail.com.

Short version
  • Meowgic works fully offline. There is no account, and we never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise (GPS) location.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Usage statistics are optional. They are collected only if you allow them, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings, which also deletes the stored usage record.
  • The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. You can remove ads with a one-time purchase.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, stars, coins, collected cats, daily streak, purchases status and settings (sound, haptics, language, consent choice) are saved in a local database on your device. This data is not uploaded to us. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

2. Optional usage statistics (only with your consent)

When you first open the App, we ask whether you want to share usage stats. If you choose Allow, we collect the information below to understand how the game is played (for example, which levels are too hard) and to improve it. If you decline, none of this is collected.

2.1 Gameplay analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

In-game events such as levels started, completed or abandoned, hints and boosters used, and screens viewed, together with app version, device model, operating system version, language and an app-instance identifier generated by Firebase. Firebase may derive an approximate region from your IP address.

2.2 Install record (Google Cloud Firestore)

A single record linked to a random, anonymous identifier created by Firebase Authentication (anonymous sign-in — no email or password). The record contains:

CategoryData
App & devicePlatform, app version and build number, operating system version, device brand and model, the store the App was installed from
Approximate locationCity, region, country and country code (see 2.3)
SettingsDevice language/locale and time zone
Game progressLevels cleared, total stars, current chapter and level
ActivityFirst install date, launch count, last active day and time

2.3 Approximate location from your IP address

To estimate your city, region and country, the App sends a request to the third-party service ipapi.co, which receives your IP address as part of any internet request. We store only the resulting city, region and country — we do not store your IP address, and we never access GPS or precise location.

2.4 Turning it off and deleting your record

You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Share usage stats. Turning it off stops collection and deletes your Firestore install record. Analytics events already sent to Google Analytics are retained under the retention setting described in section 7.

3. Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

To keep the App stable, we collect crash and error reports when the App crashes or hits an error. A report contains technical information such as the stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version, free memory/disk, a Crashlytics installation identifier and basic game state (for example, the number of levels cleared). Crash reports do not include your name, contacts or precise location. We process this data based on our legitimate interest in fixing bugs.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows ads (banner/native, interstitial, app-open and optional rewarded ads) provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your IP address, device information, app interaction data and, where available, device identifiers. Ad content is limited to family-friendly ratings.

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): we use Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent to personalized ads where required by law. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: the App does not request the Android advertising ID permission. On recent Android versions, ads may therefore be less personalized.
  • You can also reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase is valid so we can unlock the feature. Google's handling of payment information is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

6. App configuration and updates

The App uses Google Firebase Remote Config to receive settings (for example, feature switches or notice that an update is required) and Google Play's in-app update service to tell you when a new version is available. These services use a Firebase installation identifier and basic app and device information.

7. How long we keep data

  • On-device data: until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Firestore install record: until you turn off usage stats (immediate deletion) or for as long as the App is in use; inactive records are deleted after at most 24 months.
  • Google Analytics events: up to 14 months.
  • Crashlytics reports: up to 90 days.

9. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

  • Consent — usage statistics, approximate location, personalized ads.
  • Legitimate interests — crash reporting, fraud prevention, non-personalized ads that fund the free App.
  • Contract — processing your "Remove ads" purchase.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, these transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal data, to withdraw consent, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your local data-protection authority.

  • Withdraw analytics consent and delete your install record: Settings → Share usage stats.
  • Change ad consent: Settings → Ad privacy choices (where available).
  • Delete all on-device data: clear the App's storage or uninstall it.
  • Any other request: email supportvariable@gmail.com. Because we do not know who you are, we may not be able to link a request to a specific record; turning off usage stats in the App is the fastest way to delete it.

We respond to requests within 30 days.

12. Children

Meowgic is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The Firestore install record is protected by access rules so that only the device that created it can write to it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will be announced in the App where appropriate.
